
# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis  (Excel Dashboard Project)

## Overview

This project is a churn analysis performed using Microsoft Excel on a fictitious telecom provider named **Databel**. The objective was to uncover the key drivers of customer attrition and provide strategic recommendations to reduce churn.

## 🔍 What is Churn Analysis?

**Customer churn** (also known as customer attrition) refers to the rate at which customers stop using a company's product or service. Monitoring churn is essential for long-term growth because:

- Retaining customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones.
- High churn rates often indicate deeper issues such as poor service or unattractive pricing.
- Churn analysis helps businesses understand and proactively reduce customer loss.

Think of churn like a *leaky bucket*: no matter how many new customers you acquire, you’ll never grow if your current ones keep leaving.

---

## 📈 Key Insights from the Excel Dashboard

- **Overall Churn Rate**:
  - Out of 6,687 customers, 1,796 churned.
  - Resulting churn rate: **26.86%**

- **Top Churn Drivers**:
  - Competitor advantages such as **better devices** and **better offers** each represent **37%** of churn.
  - Other factors include **higher download speeds** (12%) and **more data** (14%).
  - Internal issues like **poor service**, **billing complaints**, and **lack of plan options** also contribute.

- **Demographic Impact**:
  - **Senior citizens (29%)** churn more than younger groups.
  - Most churned users fall into the **49–68 age range**.

- **State-Level Risk**:
  - **California** leads with a **75% churn rate**, followed by **Indiana (66.67%)** and **New Hampshire (62.5%)**.

- **Usage Insights**:
  - Customers using **5–10 GB/month** show higher churn, possibly due to dissatisfaction with value.

## ⚙️ Project Methodology

### 1. Data Preparation
- Cleaned and formatted customer-level data.
- Ensured data accuracy by removing duplicates and converting into Excel tables.

### 2. Churn Rate Calculation
- Converted "Churn Label" into binary values: `1` for churned, `0` for retained.
- Calculated churn rate as:
churn_rate = (Number of Churned Customers / Total Customers) * 100

### 3. Churn Reason Visualization
- Created column charts from the "Churn Reason" field to display most common reasons.

### 4. Churn Category Analysis
- Grouped reasons into broad categories (e.g., Competitor, Pricing, Support) for strategic focus.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

### 1. Compete with Better Offers
- Introduce **device trade-in programs** and **competitive bundles**.
- Develop **loyalty rewards** for long-term customers.

### 2. Improve Network Services
- Upgrade infrastructure for **faster, more reliable service**.
- Offer more **customizable data plans**, especially for 5–10 GB users.

### 3. Address Customer Service & Billing
- Enhance support team training and reduce issue resolution times.
- Improve transparency and accuracy in billing systems.

### 4. Target Senior Demographics
- Design **senior-friendly plans** with simplified options and better assistance.

### 5. Localized Retention Campaigns
- Launch **regional campaigns** in high-churn states with special deals and personalized communication.
